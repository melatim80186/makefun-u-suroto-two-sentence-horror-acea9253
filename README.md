# Two-Sentence Horror — make.fun entry

A horror story in exactly two sentences (169 characters), written for the
[Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253)
competition on make.fun.

## The story

> The dentist held up my X-ray and said I have thirty-three teeth. I counted twice on the way home, and I have thirty-two — so something in my mouth is smiling back at me.

## Why it should frighten

The horror is a counting error that cannot be explained away: the X-ray
shows thirty-three teeth, the mouth holds thirty-two, so the thirty-third
thing is not a tooth. The second sentence turns the first — a routine
check-up becomes proof that something else lives in the narrator's mouth —
and the story never says what it is. The reader fills in the worst of it.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) writes the story to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
